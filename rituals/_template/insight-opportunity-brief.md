## Draft insight

Connect the supported pattern to a meaningful human tension, need or
opportunity. Do not merely summarize the topic.

## Cultural goal

Name the quality of working life or shared behavior that may need to strengthen.

## Human-centered problem statement

Name the people and context, what they need, why it matters and an important
constraint. Do not embed a preferred solution.
