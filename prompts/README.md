# Work with the culture design team

## How the team is designed

Start with the [`TEAM-CONSTITUTION.md`](TEAM-CONSTITUTION.md). It defines the
team's shared purpose, membership, role boundaries, decision rights, working
norms and measures of effectiveness.

Every teammate uses the same [`HANDOFF-CARD.md`](HANDOFF-CARD.md), so evidence,
unknowns, disagreement and human decisions remain visible as work moves around
the team.

[`METHOD-SOURCES.md`](METHOD-SOURCES.md) records the research and ritual-design
references that informed the prompts without adding citation material to every
operational instruction.

## Recommended student path: one persistent project

For ongoing class or project work, add two materials to a ChatGPT or Claude
Project:

1. Copy the short [`Project Instructions`](../docs/downloads/Project-Instructions.txt)
   into the Project Instructions field.
2. Upload the [`Culture Design Team Guide`](../docs/downloads/Culture-Design-Team-Guide.md)
   to the Project's files, sources or knowledge area.

Start each conversation by choosing the Ethnographer, Ritual Designer,
Challenger or Experimenter. The instructions tell the AI to consult only the
selected section, so the roles do not blur together. The same setup remains
available in future conversations inside that Project.

## Guided path: one conversation

Copy all of [`culture-design-team.txt`](culture-design-team.txt) into a new
conversation with your preferred AI assistant. The team will guide you one step
at a time. You do not need to move text between teammates or manage files.

See [`../START-HERE.md`](../START-HERE.md) for what to bring, privacy guidance
and a preview of the journey.

## Studio path: four separate teammates

The self-contained prompts in [`teammates/`](teammates/) are the easiest way to
invite one specialist into an ordinary AI conversation:

- [`teammates/observer.txt`](teammates/observer.txt)
- [`teammates/designer.txt`](teammates/designer.txt)
- [`teammates/challenger.txt`](teammates/challenger.txt)
- [`teammates/experimenter.txt`](teammates/experimenter.txt)

Each prompt includes the shared purpose, the teammate's authority and boundary,
what it needs, what it contributes and how it returns the decision to a person.

The numbered files add project-file outputs for a more structured studio or
Claude Code workflow. Use them when you want to study, teach or modify the
artifact-producing method.

```text
1-ethnographer  →  real notes       →  what we observed
       HUMAN CHOOSES THE FOCUS
2-designer      →  chosen moment    →  three small practice ideas
       HUMAN CHOOSES THE IDEA
3-skeptic       →  chosen idea      →  reality check and recommendation
       HUMAN DECIDES WHETHER TO PROCEED
4-prototyper    →  approved idea    →  trial plan
       REAL PEOPLE TRY IT
4-prototyper    →  actual evidence  →  trial record
```

Start a new conversation for each teammate and paste the relevant prompt as the
first message. Pass the completed handoff card, prior result and human decision
to the next one.

If the Challenger recommends **CHANGE BEFORE TRYING**, give its one named issue
back to the Designer. After two revisions, reconsider the selected moment or
idea instead of polishing indefinitely.

## You lead the team

Every teammate completes the contribution fields and writes an `Agent caution`
about the weakest part of its own work. You complete the human checkpoint
before moving on:

```text
signed:     <name>
date:       <date>
changed:    <what I changed from the draft, and why>
decision:   <what I decided at this handoff>
reason:     <why>
distrust:   <what the next teammate should not take on faith>
```

Use the complete [`HANDOFF-CARD.md`](HANDOFF-CARD.md). `Agent caution` and human
`distrust` are deliberately separate.

The teammates can structure, imagine and challenge. Only people can observe the
workplace, secure agreement, exercise judgment and take responsibility.
