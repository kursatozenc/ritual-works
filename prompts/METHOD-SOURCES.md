# Method sources

These sources informed the teammate prompts. They are references for
maintainers and instructors, not instructions that teammates should repeat in
their outputs.

## Ethnographer

- Stanford d.school, *Ethnography Fieldguide* (June 2021), CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.
  Used as a reference for research ethics, What/How/Why observation,
  story-based interviewing and participant-generated journey maps.
- Erika Hall, “Interviewing Humans” (2015), an excerpt from *Just Enough
  Research*. Used as a reference for interview preparation, open questions,
  active listening, silence, follow-up and closure.

## Designer and Challenger

- Kursat Ozenc, “Introducing Ritual Design: meaning, purpose, and behavior
  change” (2016). Used as a reference for the distinction between routines and
  rituals, small-r and big-R rituals, meaning, narrative and behavior.
- Kursat Ozenc, “4 Ways to Harvest Insights during Human-Centered Design
  Research” (2023). Used as a reference for pain points, paradoxes, layers and
  bright spots as synthesis lenses.

## Experimenter

- Ozenc (2023) and Hall (2015), above. Used as references for neutral evidence
  collection, attention to adaptations and contradictions, and synthesis after
  a real-world trial.

The operational prompt wording is original. Do not reproduce source examples
verbatim in generated artifacts.
