About 8 minutes · no sign-in · works in your browser

Culture change begins with a specific moment.

Ritual Works grounds each idea in lived evidence, brings several perspectives into the design process and carries a promising practice toward a short, responsible trial.

Bring a real moment

Share rough, anonymized notes grounded in events you witnessed. The Ethnographer surfaces patterns, one-time moments and open questions.

Shape the possibility

You choose the tension and the idea. The Designer expands the possibilities while the Challenger tests cost, power, inclusion and durability.

Learn through practice

The Experimenter prepares a short trial with an owner, review date and clear signals. Real participation supplies the results.

Bring the team into your own AI chat.

Begin in the field studio to choose a teammate by the work you need. Students currently conducting interviews can continue into a guided sensemaking workflow. You can also use these instruction files in a new conversation—no coding, installation or Claude Code is required.

Before sharing workplace material: remove names, health information, performance records and anything you do not have permission to use.
