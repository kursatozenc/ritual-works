Four agents who share a studio: an ethnographer, a ritual designer, a skeptic and a prototyper — they go by what they do, not by call signs. They hold standup at the table, wander over to each other's desks, argue, and hand one brief around until a workplace ritual is fit to ship. Click anyone to read their charter.

Floor log

    House rules

    Seat count
    Four, and no fifth. A facilitator seat gets added the moment someone asks for one — that is how a team turns into a committee.
    Order
    Nothing skips the skeptic. A spec that goes straight from the designer to the prototyper is an intervention nobody agreed to be part of.
    Evidence
    The skeptic may block only with a named falsifier — the observation that would change its mind. A veto without one goes back to the skeptic, not to the designer.
    Retirement
    Every ritual that runs carries a retirement date. The prototyper closes the loop by walking the pilot log back to the ethnographer as next cycle's context.
