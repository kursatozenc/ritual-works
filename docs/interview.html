Build your research request

What should the Ethnographer work on?

A learning question keeps the analysis focused without deciding the answer in advance.
Good input includes what happened, when it happened, the participant’s role, what they did and what they said.
Choose one artifact for this pass

Do not paste raw recordings, names, health information, performance records, or confidential material you do not have permission to use.

How to use it

One copy, then one conversation.

  1. Paste your anonymized notes and choose one artifact.
  2. Select the permission check. The fictional example is already safe to use.
  3. Copy the complete request and paste it as the first message in a new AI conversation.
  4. Review the result with your team. Correct what is misleading, overgeneralized or missing.

Keep authorship with your team. The output is a draft interpretation—not proof, consensus, or a substitute for returning to participants.

Work carefully with partner stories.

Organizational research can contain personal and politically sensitive material. Reduce the material before bringing it into any AI tool.

Bring

  • Neutral source IDs such as N1, M1 and HR1
  • Concrete moments, actions and touchpoints
  • Short exact quotes when participants permitted their use
  • Contradictions, exceptions and positive moments

Leave out

  • Names and details that reveal identity
  • Health, immigration or performance information
  • Raw recordings and unnecessary full transcripts
  • Anything outside the agreed purpose of the research
